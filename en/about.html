About Me

About Me

I'm not just a coder — I think in terms of building. I start from a raw idea, study the problem, design a solution, and try to turn it into a real, usable product.

My career sits at the intersection of several fields: software development, artificial intelligence, product building, digital marketing, branding and entrepreneurship. That mix means I never look at a project from the code angle alone — I think about technology, product, user, experience and market at the same time. That's why I don't consider myself just a developer — I'm a Builder.

My core focus is building digital products and systems: architecture design, frontend and backend development, API design, building web applications, connecting services, deployment, testing and debugging, optimization, and MVP/prototype development. But what matters most to me is seeing the full picture of the product.

Alongside programming, I care about Product Strategy, UX, Branding, Digital Marketing, Growth and Business Models — because building a successful product isn't only about writing code. A product has to be built, usable, seen, valuable, and ultimately, it has to grow.

My Approach to AI

AI isn't just a technology to me — it's a new layer of software and user experience.

The new generation of software no longer waits for a click. It can Understand → Reason → Act. That's why AI Agents, Computer Vision, Voice AI, Automation, AI Memory, Intelligent Interfaces and Human-Computer Interaction are the areas that excite me most, and I want to help build this next generation of software.

Personal Philosophy

Problem Solving
